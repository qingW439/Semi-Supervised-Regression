Checked against the cited papers and public official repository locations on 2026-10-28. The SimRegMatch paper abstract and repository were accessible; attempts to retrieve the full `tasks/SimRegMatchTrainer.py` source hit HTTP 429. Therefore exact code-level equivalence for its filtering, interpolation coefficient, and similarity formula is **unverified**. Arm B is explicitly a fixed SimRegMatch-*style* neighbor interpolation, never presented as an official reproduction. Recheck that file before labeling an experimental arm a faithful SimRegMatch baseline.

**Proposed narrow research question:** On continuous three-dimensional EmoBank VAD, does a single label-blind RoBERTa neighborhood plus strictly OOF residuals improve a genuinely unseen TRAIN-held-out target over a frozen reference and neighbor-label interpolation? Does per-dimension residual-direction agreement improve the direct residual correction? A positive one-shot audit would justify controlled HPL integration; it would not by itself establish a novel general method. A NO-GO ends this mechanism's GPU training path.

**Source-access limit:** S2RSC and UCRC boundaries above use published metadata/abstract descriptions, not a line-by-line reproduction of their full methods. The official SimRegMatch trainer still needs code-level verification before any claimed official baseline.
